ABOUT CAREEROS

Make career decisions with evidence. Build readiness through practice.

CareerOS is an early-stage career decision and readiness platform. It helps students, graduates and career changers compare possible paths, understand the evidence a role requires, prioritize skill gaps, practise structured interviews and manage applications in one connected workspace.

Our product principles

Evidence over inflated scores

Skill ratings and readiness indicators are directional. The product should explain its inputs and never imply a guaranteed job outcome.

Action over endless advice

Career planning should end with a concrete next step, a milestone or a piece of evidence to create.

Privacy by design

Guest workspaces stay in the browser. Signed-in cloud sync uses per-user database policies. AI features require explicit user action.

Our product thesis

Most career journeys are split across role research, learning platforms, resume tools, interview preparation and job trackers. CareerOS is designed to connect those stages and make the next decision traceable to user evidence and outcomes.

What CareerOS is not

CareerOS is not a recruiter, employer, placement agency, accredited course provider or a predictor of hiring outcomes. It does not claim to scrape live job boards or provide independently verified labour-market statistics. Users should validate role requirements against current employer and industry sources.
