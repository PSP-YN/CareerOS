# Demo and acceptance checklist

## Functional walkthrough
- [ ] Set target role, experience level, timeline, objective, and project evidence.
- [ ] Save the profile and verify readiness recalculates.
- [ ] Adjust skill levels and verify gap ranking changes.
- [ ] Paste a representative job description and extract known skill keywords.
- [ ] Reorder the roadmap around the highest-priority gap.
- [ ] Mark milestones complete and verify progress persists after reload.
- [ ] Complete behavioral, technical, and HR interview practice.
- [ ] Verify feedback explains context, action, evidence, and reflection signals.
- [ ] Add an application, verify it appears, and remove it.
- [ ] Reload and verify browser-local profile, progress, interview count, and applications persist.
- [ ] If Gemini is configured, opt in and verify the optional AI feedback. Without a key, confirm local feedback remains available.

## Scoring transparency
Readiness is a directional diagnostic, not a hiring probability. The interview rubric is a practice heuristic, not a validated assessment. Skill levels are self-reported. Job-description analysis extracts a bounded list of keywords from text supplied by the user; it is not live labour-market research.

## Claims to avoid
- No guaranteed placement, offer prediction, or employer endorsement.
- No live job-market statistics or job-board scraping.
- No account system, database, or cross-device synchronization.
- No claim that Gemini is enabled until the server-side key is configured and the live endpoint is tested.
- Do not claim a perfect hackathon score or guaranteed win.
