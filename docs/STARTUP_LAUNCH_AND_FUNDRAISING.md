# CareerOS — Startup Launch and Fundraising Plan

**Prepared:** 2 November 2026  
**Current stage:** working prototype / pre-validation  
**Funding recommendation:** validate a narrow customer segment before raising equity. Explore non-dilutive incubation support only where eligibility genuinely fits.

## 1. What CareerOS is

CareerOS is an India-first career decision and readiness workspace. It connects role/offer decisions, skill-to-evidence planning, structured interview practice and job-search execution.

**Current product status:** the prototype has a role-based skill/roadmap workspace, resume builder, application tracker, seven practice categories, a local 14-question mock-interview simulation and a weighted decision scorecard. Optional Gemini coaching is consent-based and quota-limited. The scorecard and local interview rubric are aids, not validated predictors of hiring outcomes.

**Do not describe this as a production-scale business or proprietary AI model yet.**

## 2. Recommended initial market

Start with final-year engineering students, recent graduates and early-career technology candidates in India. They are easier to reach through college communities and have a recurring, time-bound problem: choosing a target role, building evidence and preparing for campus/off-campus interviews.

Avoid launching simultaneously to every job seeker, every industry and every career stage. Expand only after one segment retains and recommends the product.

## 3. Business model hypotheses

Do not turn on payments during the validation pilot. Test these models later:

1. **Candidate freemium:** free core planning and practice; paid add-ons only when they create clear repeat value and infrastructure costs are understood.
2. **College / placement-cell SaaS:** cohort dashboards, question-bank administration, placement readiness programmes and aggregated reporting. Never expose individual student answers without a clear lawful basis and consent.
3. **Bootcamp / training partner:** paid cohorts, readiness diagnostics and structured practice workflows.
4. **Career services / mentor marketplace:** optional human review, introduced only after quality, vetting and support operations exist.

Potential revenue streams are hypotheses, not current revenue.

## 4. 90-day execution plan

### Days 1–14: Product reliability and discovery
- Finish live Google sign-in, Gemini response, cross-device sync, account deletion and admin authorization tests.
- Add automated browser tests for registration, returning session, role switch, interview flow, decision scorecard, resume export and application tracking.
- Fix accessibility, mobile navigation, error recovery and empty states.
- Conduct 15–20 customer discovery interviews.
- Establish baseline event definitions and privacy-safe analytics.
- Remove unsupported marketing claims and inactive paid-plan language.

**Exit gate:** a new user can complete the core journey without founder assistance; no unresolved critical security defect.

### Days 15–30: Pilot
- Recruit 30–50 users across at least two independent communities.
- Onboard each user and observe first-session completion.
- Run one baseline mock and one follow-up mock after a practice cycle.
- Collect qualitative feedback and reasons for drop-off.
- Track weekly retention and completed meaningful actions.
- Ask users to share anonymized feedback; do not publish testimonials without permission.

**Exit gate:** repeat usage by a meaningful portion of activated users and clear evidence of the highest-value workflow.

### Days 31–60: Narrow the product
- Prioritize the three highest-friction points from pilot evidence.
- Improve question quality and role/seniority calibration.
- Add skill-to-project evidence linking and roadmap recommendations from interview gaps.
- Test a placement-cell pilot with one institution.
- Test pricing interviews and non-binding willingness-to-pay experiments.
- Publish a product changelog and transparent limitations.

**Exit gate:** at least one repeatable acquisition channel and one partner willing to run a structured pilot.

### Days 61–90: Commercial proof
- Run two or three institutional pilots with written success criteria.
- Convert one pilot to a paid letter of intent or a paid contract only if delivery and support are ready.
- Measure activation, weekly active use, completed mocks, repeat practice and cohort-level outcomes.
- Build a 12-month operating budget and use-of-funds plan.
- Prepare a data room and funding deck.

**Exit gate:** evidence of retention, user value, partner demand and a credible cost model.

## 5. Metrics investors and incubators will ask for

Define these before collecting data:

- Sign-up to activation conversion.
- Activation definition: target role selected + skill baseline completed + first action completed.
- Weekly and monthly active users.
- Week-1 and week-4 retention by cohort.
- Mock interviews started and completed.
- Repeat mock rate within 14 days.
- Average completed questions per session.
- Skill/round improvement between comparable assessments (clearly label rubric limitations).
- Roadmap milestone completion.
- Application follow-up completion.
- Referral/invitation rate.
- Cost per active user and cost per AI request.
- Support burden and critical incident rate.
- Institutional pilot conversion and renewal intent.

Do not lead with downloads, page views or an unqualified "readiness score".

## 6. Funding options in India

### A. College incubator / university entrepreneurship cell
First step for a student founder. Seek mentorship, pilot access, legal/accounting clinics, cloud credits and grant-writing support. A credible institutional pilot is more valuable than an early vanity valuation.

### B. Karnataka ELEVATE 2026 calls — urgent eligibility check
The official Startup Karnataka portal currently lists ELEVATE NxT and ELEVATE Minorities calls with an application deadline of **13 November 2026 at 5:00 PM IST**. The portal lists grant-in-aid up to ₹1 crore for ELEVATE NxT and up to ₹50 lakh for ELEVATE Minorities, subject to eligibility and milestone-based selection.

- Official portal: https://elevate.startupkarnataka.in/public/
- ELEVATE NxT is a **DeepTech** call, not a general software-startup grant. It requires a registered eligible entity, at least two people in the founding/core team, TRL 3–8 and a genuinely high-complexity, engineering-rooted innovation with IP potential. A product that simply integrates a third-party LLM API is not enough to substantiate a DeepTech claim.
- The official portal describes an anonymized qualification deck of up to two slides, a separate business-model deck and supporting company documents.
- The Minorities call has separate founder-ownership and documentation requirements. Review them only if the founders genuinely qualify; do not alter or misstate ownership to fit a grant.
- Confirm every criterion and document directly on the portal before applying. Do not rely on secondary summaries.

**Practical assessment:** CareerOS in its current form may not meet ELEVATE NxT's DeepTech bar. Apply only if you can evidence original technical R&D, a defensible innovation and the required team/registration/TRL criteria. Do not manufacture a DeepTech narrative.

### C. DPIIT Startup Recognition
Startup India states that eligible entities must be incorporated/registered in an accepted form (Private Limited, registered Partnership, LLP or cooperative society), be within the applicable age and turnover limits, and work on innovation/improvement or a scalable model with employment/wealth potential. A sole proprietorship does not qualify.

- Official eligibility and application: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startupgov/startup_recognition_page.html
- Application is routed through the National Single Window System.
- DPIIT recognition is not automatic funding and does not automatically grant every tax benefit.

### D. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme
The official SISFS FAQ describes support through selected incubators for proof-of-concept, prototype development, product trials and market entry. It requires a DPIIT-recognized startup incorporated not more than two years before application and excludes individual entrepreneurs. Confirm whether an active application window and participating incubator are available before spending time on an application.

- Official FAQ: https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/faq

### E. Angels and pre-seed funds
Approach after you have:
- a defined customer segment,
- repeat usage/retention,
- pilot evidence,
- a credible distribution channel,
- clear AI/infrastructure unit economics,
- a specific 12–18 month use of funds.

Do not raise simply to add more features. Raise to accelerate a repeatable growth or delivery engine.

## 7. Incorporation and legal checklist

Before taking institutional money, signing customer contracts or issuing equity:

- Decide the founder structure and ownership split in writing.
- Sign a founder agreement covering roles, vesting, IP assignment, confidentiality, departures and dispute resolution.
- Conduct a formal CareerOS name and trademark clearance. A similarly named India-facing product surfaced in web research; this is not a legal conclusion, but clearance is necessary.
- Discuss Private Limited vs LLP with a CA/CS and startup lawyer. Private Limited is generally more compatible with equity fundraising and ESOPs; LLP can be simpler for some bootstrapped businesses but is less convenient for conventional VC equity.
- Incorporate only when founder commitment, ownership and product direction are settled.
- Open the business bank account and establish accounting/bookkeeping.
- Obtain PAN/TAN and evaluate GST registration based on actual turnover, supplies and applicable rules.
- Execute IP assignment from founders/contractors to the entity.
- Publish privacy policy, terms, data-retention and deletion process, vendor/subprocessor disclosures and AI consent language.
- Review Indian data-protection obligations and security incident response with qualified counsel.
- Do not collect sensitive student/resume data from a college until responsibilities, consent and retention are contractually clear.
- File trademarks only after clearance and a considered class strategy.

This is a planning checklist, not legal or tax advice.

## 9. Suggested fundraising narrative

**Problem:** Early-career candidates make high-impact career decisions with fragmented information and disconnected preparation tools.

**Solution:** CareerOS connects career-path comparison, skill-to-evidence planning, structured interview practice and job-search execution in one explainable workspace.

**Why now:** AI makes personalized practice cheaper to prototype, while candidates still need a trustworthy system that distinguishes evidence from generated advice.

**Why this team:** State only verifiable founder capabilities, relevant execution and access to pilot users. Do not invent advisors, partnerships or traction.

**Ask:** Tie any funding request to specific milestones (e.g. pilot validation, reliability, institutional deployment, product research and team capacity), not a generic request to "scale AI".

## 10. Immediate founder actions

1. Recruit a committed co-founder/core team if you intend to pursue grants or build a venture beyond a solo project.
2. Schedule 15 customer interviews.
3. Find one placement-cell or student-community pilot partner.
4. Decide the initial user segment and geographic/role scope.
5. Complete production acceptance testing.
6. Confirm whether CareerOS is a name you can legally and commercially use.
7. Build a pilot cohort and publish real metrics.
8. Only then decide whether to incorporate, apply for a grant or raise equity.
